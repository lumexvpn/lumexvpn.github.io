1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the LUMEX VPN™ Android application and the related backend services required to provide VPN access, Premium activation, service notifications, support, and security.

2. Information we process

We process only information reasonably needed to operate and protect the Service:

  • Device and app data: a pseudonymous technical device identifier (LUMEX ID/HWID), manufacturer and model, operating system, app version, platform, and basic language settings.
  • Account and entitlement data: Premium code, activation state, expiry time, device slot, and free-traffic cycle information.
  • Service activity: connection timestamps, selected country or server identifier, aggregated traffic volume, connection result, and server latency/availability results.
  • Diagnostics: technical error and diagnostic information when needed to investigate failures or when you run the diagnostic feature. Diagnostic data is not intended to contain browsing content.
  • Notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, when available, so the app can receive important service and account updates.
  • Support data: information you voluntarily send when contacting support.

3. How we use information

  • provide and maintain VPN connectivity;
  • activate Premium and enforce device, time, and traffic limits;
  • select servers, measure availability, and improve reliability;
  • deliver required notices and account-status updates;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, automated attacks, and unauthorized access;
  • diagnose faults and provide user support;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Service providers and disclosure

We do not sell personal data. The minimum necessary information may be processed by hosting and network infrastructure providers, Firebase Cloud Messaging / Google Play services for notifications and app delivery, and support services. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to protect users and the Service.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices. Data may be processed in countries other than your own where our infrastructure or providers operate.

5. Data retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Service, preserve activation and anti-abuse records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Retention periods differ by record type. Data that is no longer required is deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss. No system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your LUMEX ID, subject to records we must retain for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. You may disable notifications in Android settings, but this can prevent timely delivery of important service updates.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information should contact us.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes may also be communicated in the app or through an official service channel.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact LUMEX VPN™ support on Telegram: @lumexvpn_tm_help. Include your LUMEX ID so we can locate the relevant record; do not send passwords or payment-card details.
